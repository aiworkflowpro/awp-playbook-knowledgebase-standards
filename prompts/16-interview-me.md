# 16 · Interview me

- **Purpose:** when the draft prompt is missing information only you have, let the agent ask.
  One question at a time, until all eight parts are filled.
- **In the video:** 03 Prompt standard, the interview. It appears in two forms: typed in the
  terminal, and in the conversation card.
- **Run it:** in the same session, right after prompt 15. It follows up on the previous answer,
  so a fresh session has nothing to work on.

## As shown in the video

In the terminal:

```text
Interview me to fill in what's missing. One question at a time.
```

In the conversation card:

```text
Interview me to fill in what’s missing.
```

The answers given in the video, by voice:

```text
Why reusable standards make AI agents more consistent.
```

```text
Solo AI creators who use coding agents · 90 seconds.
```

## Use it in your own project

Send it right after [prompt 15](15-prompt-from-standard-video-script.md). The prompt standard
defines an interview mode (one question at a time, with suggested options), so the agent knows
what to ask.

```text

```
