# 17 · Research before you build a Skill

- **Purpose:** before writing your own Skill, look at what already exists. The agent runs three
  searches and saves notes you can build on.
- **In the video:** 04 Skill standard, "from research to a capability of your own".

## As shown in the video

```text
Research what video generation skills are out there.
```

## Use it in your own project

Replace the topic with the Skill you want to build. It needs a web search tool.

```text
Working directory: your project root. Run pwd first and cd there if you are not in it.

Research what {topic} skills are out there. Save short notes, with links, to
research/{topic}-skill-notes.md.
```
