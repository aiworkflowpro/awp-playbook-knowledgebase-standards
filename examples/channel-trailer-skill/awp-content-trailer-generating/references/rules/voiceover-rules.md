# Voiceover Rules

Rules for the five-line, 30-second voiceover script (used by step04).

## Budgets

| Figure | Value | Why |
|--------|-------|-----|
| Narration speed | ~2.5 words per second | Natural English speaking pace |
| Total words | 60 (hard ceiling 75) | 2.5 w/s × 30 s, with 20% time held back for pauses |
| Lines | 5 — one per segment | Each segment is ~6 s |
| Words per line | ~12 (segment budget from the timeline) | A line must finish inside its window |

## Line rules

- **One sentence per line.** No semicolon chains, no parenthetical asides.
- **Hook first.** Line 1 must state the channel's promise inside the first 3 seconds. A question or a bold-but-true claim works; vague "welcome to my channel" does not.
- **Message flows through lines.** Category and promise in the hook; proof and difference in the middle; the ask at the end.
- **Plain brand voice.** Write the way the truth sheet says the brand sounds. If the brand voice is practical and structured, the lines are concrete and step-by-step, never hype.
- **Prohibited**: "subscribe for more amazing content", superlatives ("best", "incredible"), filler ("so", "basically", "you know"), and claims the truth sheet does not support.
- **End on the ask.** Line 5 names the action — subscribe, follow, watch the next video — matched to the channel.

## Freshness

- Every run generates new wording. Reusing a line from an earlier run is prohibited.
- The line must stay true to the confirmed truth sheet. If the sheet changes, the line changes.

## Read-time check (before output)

- Read each line aloud at a natural pace; it must end before its segment window closes.
- If a line runs long, cut words — do not speed up the delivery.
