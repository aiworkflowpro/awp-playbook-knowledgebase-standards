# Visual Vocabulary

The closed set of camera and editing terms the Skill may use. If a term is not here, do not use it in a shot — choose one that is here.

## Camera moves

| Term | Means |
|------|-------|
| push-in | camera moves closer to the subject |
| pull-back | camera moves away, revealing the scene |
| dolly | camera tracks sideways past the subject |
| orbit | camera circles around the subject |
| pan | camera turns left or right, staying in place |
| tilt | camera looks up or down from one spot |
| handheld | slight human wobble, documentary feel |
| rack focus | focus shifts from near subject to far subject |
| whip pan | fast blurring turn — good for scene changes |

## Shot sizes

| Term | Means |
|------|-------|
| wide | the whole scene, subject small |
| medium | person from waist up, or a desk with context |
| close-up | face, hands, or one object fills the frame |
| extreme close-up | one detail only (fingers on a key, an eye) |
| over-the-shoulder | seen from behind a person in front |

## Transitions between segments (used in step07 assembly)

| Term | Means |
|------|-------|
| hard cut | instant change |
| crossfade | dissolve through both clips |
| fade to black / from black | used for the end card |
| match cut | the next shot starts on a similar shape or motion |

## Mood words (lighting + tone)

- light: bright, soft, warm, cool, low-key, high-contrast, screen-lit, daylight, neon
- tone: calm, focused, energetic, confident, friendly, serious, hopeful

Use one light word and one tone word per shot (for example "low-key, focused").

## On-screen text

- Max four words per frame; one or two words is safest.
- Prohibited in generated frames: code blocks, terminal output, URLs, small captions, multiple lines.

## Rhythm rule

- Hook and value segments: the camera moves or the shot changes every 2-3 s.
- Proof and ask segments: one held shot with a slow push-in, so the viewer can listen.
